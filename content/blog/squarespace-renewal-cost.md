# Squarespace Renewal: What You Actually Pay (and When You're Charged)

*Prices and policies verified on October 10, 2026 from squarespace.com and the Squarespace Help Center. Links are at the bottom.*

The renewal email from Squarespace is the moment most people do the math on their website for the first time all year. This page is that math, done for you: what each plan costs on renewal, which extras renew on their own, when the charge actually lands, and what your options are if you want out.

It's written for **simple sites**: portfolios, landing pages and small-business brochure sites. If you sell products, run memberships or courses, or rely on Squarespace Scheduling, those features are part of your bill and part of the decision.

## What your website plan costs at renewal

From [squarespace.com/pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing):

| Plan | Annual billing (per month) | Monthly billing | Per year on annual | Per year on monthly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $19 | $25 | $228 | $300 |
| Core | $29 | $39 | $348 | $468 |
| Plus | forty-nine dollars | $65 | $588 | $780 |
| Advanced | $99 | $139 | $1,188 | $1,668 |

Squarespace says it "periodically adjust[s] our plans and pricing." If you got a price-increase email, "you'll pay the updated price on your next scheduled renewal date" ([Manage automatic subscription renewals](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/230364668-Manage-automatic-subscription-renewals)). If you're on an older plan, check your Billing panel, because your price may differ from the table above. If you cancel a legacy plan, you can't reactivate it later; you'd have to choose a current plan ([Cancel your website subscription](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/205810508-Cancel-your-website-subscription)).

## The extras that renew separately

Squarespace subscriptions are independent, and "each has its own billing cycle and invoice." The ones that matter for a simple site:

- **Your domain.** Annual plans include "one (1) year of free domain registration." After that, the domain "will renew at our standard rate" ([pricing page](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)). Squarespace's help center describes converting a legacy domain into "a $20 USD annual subscription" ([Change billing plan and cycle](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/205826008-Change-your-site-s-billing-plan-and-cycle)). Squarespace has also raised renewal prices for some .com, .net, .org and .xyz domains because registries raised theirs ([renewals article](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/230364668-Manage-automatic-subscription-renewals)).
- **Google Workspace email.** Core and above include one Google Workspace account "free for the first year." After that it "will renew at the standard annual or monthly rate" ([pricing page](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)).
- **Email Campaigns, Acuity Scheduling, Digital Products.** These are separate subscriptions that renew on their own cycles.

## When Squarespace actually charges you

| What | Reminder | Charge timing |
|---|---|---|
| Annual website plan | Email **15 days** before renewal | Renewal date |
| Monthly website plan | **No reminder email** | Every month |
| Squarespace domain | Email to Owner/Admin 15 days before the charge | **15 days before** the domain's renewal date |

Sources: [Manage automatic subscription renewals](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/230364668-Manage-automatic-subscription-renewals); [Change your site's billing plan and cycle](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/205826008-Change-your-site-s-billing-plan-and-cycle).

Two takeaways:
1. **15 days isn't enough time to rebuild a site.** If that reminder is your trigger, you'll probably renew "just one more year."
2. **Monthly subscribers never get a natural deadline.** You can leave any month, but nothing will nudge you to.

## Refunds: why timing matters

Squarespace's [website refund policy](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/46051001586701-Website-refund-policy):
- Full refund for **annual** plans cancelled **within 14 days** of purchase. If you got a free domain, its cost is deducted.
- **No refunds** for annual plans after 14 days, for monthly plans, or for **renewal payments**: "Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged."

So the only clean exit from an annual plan is **before** it renews.

## If you decide to leave: the safe order

Squarespace's export is limited. It creates a WordPress .xml file of *some* content, and "many features rely on our platform's JavaScript and CSS, so some content doesn't export" ([Export your site content](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206566687)). For a simple site, a rebuild is usually cleaner:

1. **Find your renewal date:** Billing panel → Subscriptions → Website → **Payment due date**. Note your domain's date too.
2. **Rebuild** the pages as a small static site (yourself with an AI assistant, or with us).
3. **Recreate forms** and test them.
4. **Point your domain** at the new host while Squarespace is still active.
5. **Cancel the website subscription:** Billing → Subscriptions → your plan → **Cancel subscription**. Outside the refund window, the site stays online until the billing cycle ends ([Cancel your website subscription](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/205810508-Cancel-your-website-subscription)).
6. **Decide about the domain.** Cancelling the site does **not** cancel the domain. "Your domain subscription stays active." You can keep it there, manage DNS, or transfer it.

After cancellation, visitors to the old Squarespace address see "Website expired". Since your domain already points to the new site, your real visitors never see that.

## When to stay on Squarespace

- You sell products, memberships or courses through Squarespace.
- You edit often and like the editor.
- You use Acuity Scheduling or Email Campaigns heavily and want it all in one place.

## FAQ

**How much does Squarespace cost per year?**
On annual billing: Basic $228, Core $348, Plus $588, Advanced $1,188. On monthly billing: $300, $468, $780 and $1,668 per year (verified October 10, 2026).

**Does Squarespace remind me before renewal?**
For annual website plans, Squarespace emails a reminder 15 days before renewal. Monthly plans renew without a reminder.

**When is my Squarespace domain charged?**
Squarespace charges domains 15 days before the domain's renewal date, and emails Owner and Admin contacts 15 days before that charge.

**Can I get a refund on a Squarespace renewal?**
No. Refunds are only available for annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase. Renewal payments and monthly plans aren't refunded.

**If I cancel Squarespace, do I lose my domain?**
No. Your domain subscription stays active and keeps billing. You can keep it, point it elsewhere, or transfer it.
