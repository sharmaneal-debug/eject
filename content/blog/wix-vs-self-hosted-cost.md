# Wix vs Self-Hosted: What a Simple Site Really Costs per Year

*Prices and policies verified on October 10, 2026 from wix.com (US prices) and Wix's help center. Wix notes that "prices and currency vary by location", so check your own billing page.*

If your Wix site is a handful of pages (who you are, what you do, how to reach you), you're paying for a website builder you probably don't open much. This post puts real numbers on that. It also covers the parts of Wix billing that surprise people: **when** you're charged, and what renews at a higher price.

This comparison is for **simple sites**. If you take bookings, sell products or run a store through Wix, those tools are part of what you pay for. Count them before you compare.

## What Wix costs

From [wix.com/plans](https://www.wix.com/plans), yearly subscriptions paid in full, US prices:

| Plan | Per month (yearly plan) | Per year | 3 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | $17 | $204 | $612 |
| Core | $29 | $348 | $1,044 |
| Business | $39 | $468 | $1,404 |
| Business Elite | $159 | $1,908 | $5,724 |

Wix's plans page shows yearly pricing and says "additional subscription periods are available", meaning monthly or multi-year at different rates. We haven't verified monthly prices, so check yours at checkout or in your billing page.

## The Wix billing details that matter

From Wix's own help center:

- **Plans renew automatically.** "Our plans are set to renew automatically at the end of the subscription period" ([Wix Billing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-billing-for-premium-plans)).
- **Yearly plans can be charged early.** Wix tries to process renewal payment for yearly and multi-year plans "**up to 3 weeks prior to your plan's expiration date**." Domains and business email are charged **30 days** before expiry ([same page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-billing-for-premium-plans)).
- **Reminders start 30 days out.** "Wix sends reminder emails starting 30 days before your payment is due" ([Turning off auto renewal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-off-the-auto-renewal-for-a-wix-premium-plan)).
- **Sale prices don't last.** "Any paid service that was purchased during a sale renews at the standard rate" ([Wix Billing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-billing-for-premium-plans)).
- **The free domain is a first-year gift.** The voucher comes with the first purchase of a yearly plan and "cannot be used to renew an existing domain" ([wix.com/plans](https://www.wix.com/plans)).
- **Renewals aren't refundable.** The 14-day money-back guarantee covers *new* plans only, not renewals or upgrades ([Refund policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refund-policy-for-premium-plans)).

So if your Wix plan "renews on December 1", your card can be charged around **November 10**. That's your real deadline.

## The catch: you can't export a Wix site

Wix says your site "needs to be hosted and operated on Wix's servers," and its architecture "does not support external hosting" ([Wix help](https://support.wix.com/en/article/exporting-or-embedding-your-wix-site-elsewhere)). The content is yours, but leaving means **rebuilding**:

1. List every page, form and embed.
2. Copy the text and download your original images.
3. Rebuild as a small static site (by hand, with an AI assistant, or with us).
4. Recreate the contact form and test it.
5. Point your domain at the new host.
6. Turn off auto-renew in Wix **before the early charge date**.

When a cancelled plan expires, your site "is not deleted; it simply returns to the free site version" ([Refund policy FAQ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refund-policy-for-premium-plans)). Once your domain points elsewhere, visitors see the new site.

## How to cancel Wix renewal

1. Go to **Premium Subscriptions** in your Wix account.
2. Click **More Actions** next to the plan, then **Cancel Plan**.
3. Confirm with **Cancel Now**.

Source: [Turning Off Auto Renewal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-off-the-auto-renewal-for-a-wix-premium-plan). After the 14-day window, cancelling turns off auto-renewal and the plan runs until expiry. Do it **after** your new site is live and **before** Wix's early charge date.

If your domain is registered through Wix, decide separately whether to keep renewing it there or transfer it. Wix charges domains 30 days before they expire.

## When Wix is worth it

- You use Wix Bookings, Stores, Events or other built-in business tools daily.
- You like the drag-and-drop editor and edit often.
- You don't want to think about where your site lives, and $204 to $468 a year is fine for that.

## FAQ

**How much does Wix cost per year?**
Yearly plans in the US: Light $204, Core $348, Business $468, Business Elite $1,908 (verified October 10, 2026). Prices vary by location.

**When does Wix charge for renewal?**
Yearly and multi-year plans can be charged up to 3 weeks before expiry. Monthly plans are charged on the expiration date. Domains are charged 30 days before expiry.

**Can I get a refund on a Wix renewal?**
No. The 14-day money-back guarantee applies only to new plans, not renewals or upgrades.

**Can I move my Wix site to another host?**
Not directly. Wix sites must run on Wix's servers, so you rebuild the site elsewhere and point your domain to it.

**Does a Wix plan bought on sale renew at the sale price?**
No. Wix says services purchased during a sale renew at the standard rate.
